Our Story

Where every meal is an invitation.

In Afghan homes, food is hospitality. A pot of mantu on the table is a quiet sentence: stay, eat, you belong here. Jummah Foods began in a family kitchen, three generations of women folding dumplings on Friday afternoons, the air warm with garlic and dried mint.

We made Jummah to bottle that feeling. To bring the slow craft of an Afghan grandmother's table to a freezer aisle near you, without compromise on taste, texture, or tradition.

How It Works

From freezer
to feast in minutes.

A premium dinner that respects your time. Three quiet steps between a long day and a long table.

01

Heat

Steam, simmer, or microwave straight from frozen. Eight minutes from tray to table, no thawing, no fuss.

02

Plate

Pour over the included garlic yogurt sauce. Crown it with the herb or beef topping and a generous shower of dried mint.

03

Gather

Bring the platter to the middle of the table. Pour tea. Make space for one more chair. This is what the food was made for.
